Technical University of Košice · 2026 · Master's thesis (Ing.)

Control of a laboratory plant using an advanced control algorithm

A bell-type laboratory furnace and three ways of holding its temperature: IMC-PID, dynamic matrix control (DMC) and model algorithmic control (MAC). I identified the system experimentally, designed each controller against that model, compared them in Matlab and Simulink, then ran all three on the real furnace by varying its power.

In simulation MAC settled best and DMC worst — it overshot and oscillated. On the real furnace, judged on the temperature actually measured rather than the model of it, IMC-PID held it steadiest. The simulation and the hardware did not agree, and that gap is the interesting part.

Matlab · Simulink · PLC · experimental identification · 88 pages

Technical University of Košice · 2024 · Bachelor's thesis

Design and implementation of an advanced control method

An air heater held at a set temperature by dynamic matrix control. The algorithm ran in Matlab; the OPC toolbox carried it across to a PLC, which drove a circuit of an adjustable potentiometer, the heater itself and a thermocouple. DMC read the measured temperature and regulated fan power to hold the setpoint.

Theory first, then a simulation model, then the same algorithm on real hardware — with the wiring diagram and the real-time results in the closing chapters. It is where the loop from equation to running equipment closed for the first time.

Matlab · OPC · PLC · DMC · 56 pages
